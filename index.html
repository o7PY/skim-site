Your course’s Teams, skimmed into cards.

Skim reads your course team’s posts and files, spots the dates, and turns them into a swipeable deck with reminders and change alerts.

How it works

  1. 1

    Your professor adds Skim

    The Skim bot is added to the course team once. It quietly records posts and files.

  2. 2

    The bot posts a code

    A pairing code appears in the team, so only people in the course get it.

  3. 3

    Enter it in the app

    Type the code in Skim and the course shows up as cards.

What it does today

Every kind of event has its own colour, in the app and here.

  • Quiz
  • Exam
  • Assignment
  • Project
  • Lab
  • Class
  • Office hours
  • Presentation
  • Other

  • One deck for every course

    Real Teams messages, files and date changes from all your courses, in one swipeable stack.

  • Swipe to decide

    Swipe left to dismiss (with undo). Swipe right to open the full card.

  • Open in Teams, open the file

    Every card links back to the original message and any attached file.

  • Dates, spotted

    Dates appear as chips. If Skim is not sure, the card says "Unconfirmed".

  • Reminders that follow the date

    Recommended or custom reminders. When a date moves, your reminder moves with it.

  • Change alerts

    A notification when a date, time or venue changes.

  • Stars and temporary stars

    Keep a card in Starred, or star it for a day or a week.

  • Snooze

    Bring a card back later, when you can deal with it.

  • Add to your calendar

    Send an event to your phone calendar in one tap.

  • The Important checklist

    Pick the kinds of event that matter. They stand out in red.

  • Quiet hours, mute, urgent only

    Silence a course, set quiet hours, or let only urgent alerts through.

  • Works offline

    Your latest cards stay readable without a connection.

Privacy, plainly

  • What Skim stores about you: your Google email and name, your stars, reminders and swipe state, and your phone’s notification token.
  • Professors see nothing about students. There is no professor view of what you read or starred.
  • Delete your account any time in Settings. Everything stored about you is removed.
  • Private channels are never read. Skim only follows the standard channels of teams its bot was added to.
  • No trackers on this site. No analytics, no cookies, no third-party requests.

FAQ

Why Android only?

Skim is an early preview built and tested on Android first. An iPhone version is not available yet.

How do I install an APK?
  1. Open this page on your Android phone and tap Download for Android (APK).
  2. When the download finishes, open the file. If Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps (Settings → Apps → your browser → Install unknown apps).
  3. Tap Install, then open Skim and sign in with Google.
  4. You can switch the unknown-apps permission off again afterwards.
Does my professor need to do anything?

Yes, once: add the Skim bot to the course team. After that, nothing more. Students join with the code the bot posts.

Is this official IISc software?

No. Skim is an independent early preview. It is not offered, endorsed or supported by IISc or Microsoft.

What if the date in a message is wrong?

Skim can misread a message. That is why every card always shows the original message, and a date it is unsure of is marked Unconfirmed. Open in Teams shows you the source.

For professors

Skim is not open for course installs yet; it is in early testing. If you would like to know more, or try it with a course, please get in touch.
