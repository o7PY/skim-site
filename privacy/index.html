Privacy policy

Skim 1.0.0 · early preview. This page describes what the Skim app and its service actually do today.

Who we are

Skim is an independent early-preview project, not official IISc or Microsoft software. Contact: pragnyayadav22@gmail.com.

What we collect, and why

  • Your Google account email and name, from Google sign-in, to identify you and show your account.
  • Your Skim activity: the courses you joined, which cards you dismissed or opened, your stars, snoozes and reminders, and your settings (quiet hours, mute, new-post alerts, important kinds). This makes the app work across your devices and is never shown to anyone else.
  • Your push token: an identifier from Expo’s push service for your phone, used only to send you reminders and alerts (a date that moved, a new post or file). Notification text holds course and event details only, never people’s names or message text.
  • Course content: when a professor adds the Skim bot to a course team, Skim records the posts, edits, deletions, reactions and file changes in that team’s standard channels, and reads files’ text, to build cards. Private and shared channels are never read.
  • Your questions to “Ask the course”: a question and the relevant course text are sent, with names, emails, phone numbers and roll numbers replaced, to a language-model provider to write the answer. The question and the answer are not stored on our server; we keep only the time of each question, to limit how many can be asked per hour.

Skim does not collect your location, contacts, or anything else from your phone. The only phone permissions asked for are notifications, exact alarms for reminders, and writing an event to your calendar when you ask for it.

Who can see what

Professors and course staff cannot see which cards you read, starred, dismissed or asked about, or any of your settings. There is no professor view of student activity. Joining a course needs the code posted in its team, and message author names are shown as Teams shows them.

Where it is stored

  • Account data, course content and your activity are stored in a database on Supabase.
  • The Skim service runs on Google Cloud (Cloud Run, Mumbai region).
  • Your phone also keeps a copy of your latest cards and your settings for offline use.

Third parties

  • Google sign-in verifies who you are (Google’s own policy applies to that step).
  • Expo’s push service delivers notifications to your phone.
  • Language-model providers process redacted course text to find dates and categories, and to answer your questions.
  • Nothing is sold, and nothing is used for advertising. This website has no analytics, cookies or third-party requests.

How long we keep it

Your account data is kept until you delete your account. Course content is kept while the Skim bot remains in that team. If you leave a course in the app, the cards, history, stars and reminders you had for it are removed from your account.

Deleting your data

In the app, open Settings → Delete account and confirm by typing the word shown. This removes your subscriptions, activity, reminders, push tokens and your sign-in account at once. You can also write to the contact address above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published here with a new date and app version.
